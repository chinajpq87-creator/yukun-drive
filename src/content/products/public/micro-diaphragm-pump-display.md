# Micro Diaphragm Pump

Micro diaphragm pumps can be considered for compact air or liquid movement where media compatibility, flow behavior, pressure, noise, and package size must be balanced.

## Typical Applications

- Analytical and laboratory equipment
- Beverage and dispensing equipment
- Environmental monitoring
- Portable fluid-handling systems

## Selection Inputs

Provide the working media, desired flow and pressure, supply, duty cycle, installation orientation, acoustic target, and tubing interface.
