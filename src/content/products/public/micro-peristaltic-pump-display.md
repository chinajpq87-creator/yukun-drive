# Micro Peristaltic Pump

Peristaltic pumping can be considered when the fluid path should remain within replaceable tubing and controlled dosing or transfer is required.

## Typical Applications

- Laboratory analyzers
- Reagent handling
- Compact dosing equipment
- Cleaning and dispensing systems

## Selection Inputs

Share the media, target flow range, tubing material, duty cycle, direction control, package envelope, and cleaning or replacement method.
