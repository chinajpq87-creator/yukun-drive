Smart-lock drive selection depends on the lock architecture, load, available space, power source, motion sequence, feedback, and user-experience targets.

## Drive Categories to Consider

- Spur-gear motors for straightforward compact transmission
- Planetary-gear motors for centered packaging and controlled motion
- Worm-gear motors where right-angle output or holding behavior matters
- Feedback options for position-aware control
